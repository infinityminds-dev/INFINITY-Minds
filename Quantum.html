🧠 Quantum Concepts

What is Quantum?

Uses quantum physics principles to solve complex problems faster than traditional computers.

Qubit

The basic unit of quantum computing. It can be 0, 1, or both at the same time.

Superposition

Allows a qubit to exist in multiple states at once, increasing computing power exponentially.

Entanglement

Connects qubits so that changing one instantly affects another, even over long distances.

Quantum Gates

Manipulate qubits to perform operations, similar to logic gates in classical computers.

Classical vs Quantum

Classical uses bits (0 or 1), while quantum uses qubits for parallel processing.
